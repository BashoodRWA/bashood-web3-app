## 🏝️ OPTION 1: CAYMAN FOUNDATION COMPANY (RECOMMENDED)

### Overview
Cayman Islands Foundation Company es la estructura más popular para proyectos crypto internacionales. Usado por: Ethereum Foundation, Tezos Foundation, 250+ crypto projects.

### Key Features
✅ **Tax Advantages:**
- 0% corporate tax
- 0% capital gains tax
- 0% withholding tax
- No VAT/sales tax

✅ **Regulatory Benefits:**
- No registration requirements para token issuance
- No securities registration (if utility token)
- VASP license opcional (si custody/exchange features)
- CIMA oversight (credibility internacional)

✅ **Structure:**
- Foundation = Non-profit entity
- Can receive donations, issue tokens
- Controlled by "Supervisory Committee"
- No shareholders (no equity dilution)

✅ **CEX Listings:**
- Accepted by: Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, all major CEX
- Required docs: Certificate of Incorporation, M&A, Good Standing
- KYC: Directors/supervisors must pass exchange KYC

### Costs

✅ **Reputation:**
- Switzerland = premium jurisdiction
- Strong legal system (civil law)
- Crypto-friendly regulation (FINMA)
- MiCA compliance pathway

✅ **Tax:**
- 0-8% corporate tax (depending on canton)
- Geneva, Zug: 12-15% effective
- Ticino, Schwyz: 8-10%
- Tax rulings available (negotiate with canton)

✅ **Banking:**
- Best banking access in crypto (SEBA, Sygnum, UBS, Credit Suisse)
- IBAN account (EU SEPA accepted)

✅ **Structure:**
