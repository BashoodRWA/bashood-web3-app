## 🎨 DESIGN GUIDELINES

### Visual Style:
- **Colors:** Blue (trust) + Gold (premium) + White (clean)
- **Fonts:** Sans-serif (Montserrat, Inter, or Helvetica)
- **Icons:** Minimalist, consistent style (Feather Icons, Heroicons)
- **Layout:** Lots of white space, not cluttered

### Do's:
✅ Use data/metrics (builds credibility)
✅ Use comparisons (Ondo, Backed, Centrifuge)
✅ Use visuals (charts, screenshots, mockups)
✅ Keep text minimal (bullets, not paragraphs)
✅ Tell a story (Problem → Solution → Opportunity → Ask)

### Don'ts:
❌ No walls of text (max 5 bullets per slide)
❌ No fancy animations (professional, not PowerPoint 2003)
❌ No jargon (explain DeFi terms if using)
❌ No over-promising (be realistic)
❌ No typos (proofread 3x)
